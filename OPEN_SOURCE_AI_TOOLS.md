## ✅ Selection Rule

Projects are listed because their source code is publicly available under an open-source license. Model weights used with these projects are separate works and must be checked individually for their own license.

## ❌ Not Included

Paid SaaS, trial-only services, subscription-only products, proprietary hosted AI, and projects with non-open-source/custom restrictive licenses are intentionally excluded.
